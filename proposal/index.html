Admin command center

Less waiting on a webpage provider.

The admin side is designed to give I Do Transportation more control over the site without waiting on a third party for every text or photo change. The demo includes a secure login, editable content fields, media controls, and a visual editor path for direct on-page changes.

  • Update key homepage copy and brand colors.
  • Swap hero, package, and fleet images.
  • Preview the site through the admin dashboard.
  • Use a floating visual editor for direct page-level edits.

Important demo note

The admin workflow is a functional prototype connected to a local demo backend for review. Production would include final hosting, role/password hardening, backups, deployment rules, analytics, and a safer content publishing workflow.

AI concierge direction

Helpful first response, not overpromised automation.

The AI chatbot direction is focused on practical support: answering common questions, qualifying quote requests, deflecting pricing until the route is understood, and preparing a clean handoff for the human team. More advanced voice receptionists and text-back systems can be treated as future Phase 2 upgrades.

  • FAQ and quote-start assistant for wedding and event transportation.
  • Phone-number capture for fast follow-up.
  • Pricing deflection so the business keeps control of custom quotes.
  • Future-ready structure for SMS or Make.com style workflow handoff.

Next step questionnaire

If you want to move forward.

To begin the full build, we would collect a 50% deposit and then use this questionnaire to finish the site around your exact preferences, packages, policies, photos, and launch goals.

  1. What are the top three services you want visitors to book?
  2. Which wedding venues should be featured first?
  3. What are your exact service areas and airport coverage?
  4. What vehicle types, capacities, and amenities should be listed?
  5. Which photos are final, and which should be replaced or upgraded?
  6. What tone should the copy use: luxury, friendly, family-owned, or direct?
  7. What phone number and email should receive leads?
  8. Do you want form submissions, texts, emails, or all three?
  9. Which current website problems are most frustrating?
  10. Who should have admin access after launch?
  11. Do you want the chatbot included in Phase 1 or saved for Phase 2?
  12. What launch timeline feels realistic?

This is a complete mockup and working demo direction, not the finished production website. It is intentionally still in your hands. We can keep the current direction, simplify it, make it more formal, make it more family-owned, or take it in another direction before launch.
