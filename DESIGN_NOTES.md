# Design Notes

## Skills Applied

- `ui-ux-pro-max`: accessibility, touch targets, reduced motion, responsive checks, and design-system framing
- `frontend-design`: distinctive aesthetic direction, memorable visual anchor, production-grade static implementation
- `page-cro`: one clear primary action, trust proof before repeated CTA, objection handling through packages/process
- `form-cro`: short first-step inquiry form with visible labels, mobile-friendly fields, and outcome-based submit copy
- `seo-structure-architect`: single H1, logical H2/H3 flow, LocalBusiness JSON-LD, venue/service keyword structure
- `ui-visual-validator`: screenshot-based verification targets across desktop and mobile

## CRO Diagnosis of Current Site

Estimated conversion readiness: **52 / 100**

Main constraints:

- The value proposition is factual but not shaped around visitor outcomes.
- Packages are not scannable as decision paths.
- Venue familiarity is buried as a plain list instead of used as trust proof.
- CTAs repeat, but they do not explain what happens after clicking.
- The contact path is too visually quiet for a high-intent booking site.

## New Funnel

1. Hero: immediate wedding/event promise with venue slideshow and primary CTA
2. Proof: years in business, capacity, airport coverage, award badges
3. Reframe: explain the planning experience
4. Packages: route visitors by intent
5. Fleet: show the vehicle experience clearly
6. Venues: convert local venue knowledge into trust
7. Process: explain the booking path
8. Reserve: short lead form with direct contact backup

## Visual Direction

DFII estimate: **13 / 15**

- Aesthetic impact: 4
- Context fit: 5
- Implementation feasibility: 5
- Performance safety: 4
- Consistency risk: 5

The site avoids a generic transportation template by making the venue-and-itinerary story the main design anchor, not just placing vehicle photos in ordinary cards.
